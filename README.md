## Featured projects

| Project | Summary | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| [American University Basketball Expected Points Dashboard](https://github.com/franciscorezzonico/au_basketball_expected_points) | Developed an interactive dashboard for American University’s men’s basketball coaching staff to explore team and player-specific expected points by shot zone, shot type, and defensive contest level. Integrated foul-adjusted predictions, shot-frequency-weighted KPIs, configurable inclusion thresholds, and limited-sample warnings. Published a live demonstration using fictitious player names and data to protect privacy. | R, Shiny, logistic regression, weighted linear regression, ggplot2, Posit Connect Cloud |
| [European Football Transfer Market Analysis](https://github.com/franciscorezzonico/european-football-transfer-market-analysis) | Built a reproducible analytical workflow using 70,006 football transfer records across seven European leagues from 2009–2021, producing a cleaned sample of 7,203 paid incoming transfers. Evaluated league-level fee differences, nonlinear age effects, positional pricing, and long-run fee inflation. Developed an interactive Tableau dashboard to explore transfer expenditure, transaction volume, and fee patterns by season, league, age, and position. | R, Quarto, Tableau, dplyr, ggplot2, OLS regression, quadratic regression, ANOVA, log-linear modeling |
| [Manhattan Private-Room Pricing Analysis](https://github.com/franciscorezzonico/manhattan_private_room_pricing_analysis) | Built a reproducible Quarto workflow to evaluate how service fees, review volume, cancellation policies, and minimum-stay requirements are associated with nightly private-room prices across five Manhattan neighborhoods. The analysis includes data cleaning, exploratory analysis, multiple linear regression, diagnostic testing, effect-size estimation, and an interactive visualization. | R, Quarto, multiple linear regression, ggplot2, Plotly, statistical diagnostics |
| [EV Make and CAFV Eligibility Analysis](https://github.com/franciscorezzonico/ev_cafv_elegibility_analysis) | Analyzed 103,032 Washington State EV registrations to assess the association between vehicle make and Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle eligibility. Applied descriptive analysis, a chi-square test of independence, Cramér’s V, and a 100% stacked bar chart to communicate results and data limitations. | R, dplyr, ggplot2, chi-square test, Cramér’s V, data visualization |
| [Spotify Music Market Analysis](https://github.com/franciscorezzonico/spotify_analysis) | Examined 9,227 Spotify tracks released from 2020–2023 to identify song and artist characteristics associated with track popularity and translate descriptive patterns into strategic opportunities for music producers. | R, dplyr, ggplot2, Plotly, exploratory data analysis, market analytics |
